Polo school and coaching

200€lesson with horse

Private and group lessons for all levels. Whether you have never sat on a horse or you are a club player looking to sharpen your game, we teach the way we learned: with patience, on good horses, and with real polo as the goal from the start. Coaching covers stick and ball technique, game strategy, horsemanship and match preparation. Every session is tailored to where you are and where you want to go. We do not believe in one size fits all. We believe in reading the rider and building from there.
